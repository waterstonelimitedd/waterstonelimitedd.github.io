Lunar Foundry

Privacy Policy — effective 2026-10-29

1. Introduction

Welcome to Lunar Foundry! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how WATERSTONE LIMITED (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects information in connection with your use of Lunar Foundry (“the App”).

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of data when you use the App:

  • Device Information (e.g., device model, operating system, device identifiers)
  • Purchase information — if you buy an item in the App, Google Play sends the App a record of that purchase (order number, the item bought, the time of purchase and a purchase token). We never receive your payment card, bank or billing details.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App
  • Diagnose technical issues
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Deliver and restore the items you buy, and prevent fraudulent or duplicate purchases

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain collected data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above, and it is automatically deleted within 90 days. You may also request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us using the details below, and we will process your request promptly. Purchase records the App receives are kept on your device only for as long as the App is installed; Google keeps its own payment records under Google's policies (see the In-App Purchases section).

5. Advertising

Lunar Foundry does not display advertising and does not share your data with advertising networks for ad targeting.

6. In-App Purchases

Lunar Foundry is free to play and offers optional in-app purchases of virtual coins. All payments are processed by Google Play (Google LLC): your payment details are handled by Google under the Google Payments Privacy Notice and the Google Privacy Policy, and are never shared with us.

  • What we receive: only the purchase record described in the Information We Collect section, which the App uses to add the coins to your balance and to finish purchases that were interrupted. It is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising.
  • Virtual coins have no real-money value, cannot be exchanged for cash and can only be used inside the App. They are stored on your device: uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes them.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policies.
  • Parents: every purchase needs the approval of the Google account holder. You can require a password or approval for purchases in the Google Play app (Settings → Authentication, or Google Family Link).

7. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for analytics and crash reporting (for example, to measure app performance and fix bugs). These providers process data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal data.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at waterstonelimitedd@gmail.com.
